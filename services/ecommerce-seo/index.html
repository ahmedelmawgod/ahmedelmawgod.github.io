Why online stores need a different approach

A store has hundreds or thousands of pages that look alike: categories, filters, and products with similar names. Without a plan, these pages compete with each other and Google ranks none of them well.

What I do for online stores

  • Keyword mapping. I match every important search to one page, so each category and product has a clear job.
  • Category and collection pages. These usually carry the biggest searches. I optimize their titles, headings, and copy.
  • Product pages. Titles and descriptions that match how people search for the product, plus product schema.
  • Internal linking. Links between related categories and products so authority reaches the pages that sell.
  • Titles and meta descriptions. Search results that give people a reason to click.
  • Technical health. Crawl efficiency, duplicate filter URLs, and page speed. See technical SEO.
