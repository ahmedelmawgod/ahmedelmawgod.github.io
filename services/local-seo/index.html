What local SEO includes

  • Google Business Profile. Setting up and completing the profile for each branch: categories, services, hours, photos, and contact details.
  • Local pages. Website pages that match what people near each branch search for.
  • Consistent business details. The same name, address, and phone number across the website and directories.
  • Local links. Backlinks from relevant local websites, found through outreach and competitor research.
  • Schema markup. Local business structured data so Google understands where you are and what you offer.
