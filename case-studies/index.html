Esterad

Electronics eCommerce, Egypt

+689% clicksin 3 months

MetricBeforeAfter
Clicks1.61K12.7K
Impressions50K291K
Average position13.97.6

Esterad sells electronics online in Egypt. When I started, its average position in Google was 13.9, which is page two, where very few people click.

What I did

  • Keyword mapping. I matched each important search to one page, so category pages and product pages stopped competing for the same keywords.
  • On-page optimization. I rewrote titles, headings, and page copy around the search each page was mapped to.
  • Internal linking. I linked related categories and products so authority flowed to the pages that sell.

The average position moved from 13.9 to 7.6, which is the difference between page two and page one of Google.

Rakiza

Health and beauty eCommerce, Saudi Arabia

+89% organic revenuewith clicks doubled in 3 months

MetricBeforeAfter
Organic clicks–2x in 3 months
Impressions–+900K
Clicks, last 12 months–181K

Rakiza is a health and beauty store in Saudi Arabia. The goal was not only more traffic, but more sales from Google.

What I did

  • Keyword strategy. I focused on the product and category searches with buying intent.
  • Product and collection pages. I optimized titles, descriptions, and on-page content for the pages that carry revenue.
  • Technical fixes. I cleaned up the issues that stopped Google from crawling and indexing the store properly.

Clicks doubled in three months and organic revenue grew by 89 percent.

KAZA Stores

Electronics eCommerce, Egypt

+54% clicksin 3 months

MetricBeforeAfter
Clicks19.1K29.5K
Click-through rate0.7%1.0%
Clicks, last 6 months–198K

KAZA Stores already had strong visibility, so the opportunity was to win more clicks from the impressions it was already getting, and to make the site easier and faster for Google to crawl.

What I did

  • Titles and meta descriptions. I rewrote them so the search result gave people a clearer reason to click. CTR rose by 43 percent.
  • Crawl efficiency. I reduced wasted crawling so Google spent its time on the pages that matter.
  • Server performance. I worked on response times so pages load faster for both users and Googlebot.

Max Gym

Fitness center and gym chain

260+ qualified leadsper month

MetricBeforeAfter
Clicks1,6503,050
Impressions69.4K179K
Time–3 months

Max Gym is a gym chain. For a local business, success is measured in people who call, message, or visit, so the work focused on local search and on turning visits into leads.

What I did

  • Local SEO. I set up and optimized Google Business Profiles for the branches.
  • On-page SEO. I aligned the website pages with what people near each branch search for.

The site now brings in more than 260 qualified leads every month.
